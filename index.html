    03 / OUR APPROACH

    Customer trust shouldn't be an afterthought.

    PolicyReply is an early-stage, bootstrapped product experiment for independent sellers and small teams. We believe customer support drafts should cite the business's own rules and make uncertainty visible.

    Our public prototype uses deterministic text matching, not a language model. We are preparing an optional Claude API integration to improve drafting and policy reasoning when properly configured. We do not claim Anthropic partnership or sponsorship.

    ✳

    Grounded by design

    If a question has no matching policy, the demo does not invent one. It asks for human follow-up instead.
